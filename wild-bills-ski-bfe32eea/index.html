Our story

A family-run shop at the foot of the Face.

Wild Bill's has been in business since the early 1980s and has been owned by K Bolan since 1993. Certified technicians fit every boot, and every rental is inspected and tuned before it leaves the counter. The goal is simple: be your one-stop shop, from first-timers to experts.

“Very good price and the workers were extremely helpful and nice. Ski and Snowboard boots fit perfect and it was a lot of fun.”Yelp reviewer, March 2025
What we do

Vacation rentals

Sleep a half-block from the Platinum chair.

Second-floor units overlooking the Face of the ski area — watch the torchlight parade and fireworks from your front door. Kitchens, flat-screen TVs, WiFi, small pets welcome by arrangement. Rates from $155–$225 per night as posted; room rates vary by season.
